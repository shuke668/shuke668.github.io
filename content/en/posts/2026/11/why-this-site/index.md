The scarce resource is not news. It is **filtering** and **judgement**.

This site does three things:

1. Collect entries across technology, world affairs, political economy and defense;
2. Keep the headline, a short excerpt and a link — never the full text;
3. Add a point of view only where it deserves one.

## Why build it myself

Platforms rank for their own goals, not necessarily yours. Owning the site means taking back both what you read and how you think about it.

## Three layers

- **Briefs** — fetched by machine, covering "what is new";
- **Essays** — written by hand, explaining "why";
- **Picks** — chosen manually, expressing "what matters".

Separating the three keeps publishing frequency from holding content quality hostage.

> Information is not knowledge. Judgement is.
