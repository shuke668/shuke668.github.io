A personal news desk covering technology, world affairs, political economy and defense.

Three layers: auto-aggregated briefs, manually selected picks, and hand-written essays. Aggregated entries keep only the headline and a short excerpt; all rights belong to the original publishers.

Built with Hugo. Writing happens in Obsidian or Typora. A push to GitHub publishes it.
